## 🛡️ SHIELD / BLOCK SYSTEM

### How Shields Work:

- **Shield Health**: 100% (green bar in HUD)
- **Blocks ALL attacks** when active
- **Depletes** while holding (0.3% per frame)
- **Regenerates** when not blocking (0.5% per frame = 30/sec)
- **Shield Break**: At 0%, you get 2 seconds of stun!

### Shield Controls:

#### PS5 Controller:

- **L1 (Left Bumper)** = Hold to shield
- **L2 (Left Trigger)** = Hold to shield

#### Keyboard:

- **P1**: Hold **S** key
- **P2**: Hold **Down Arrow**

### Shield Visual:

- Blue bubble appears around character
- Bubble transparency shows health
- When hit, shield absorbs damage instead of you!

### Shield Strategy:

✅ **Block attacks to avoid knockback**
✅ **Regenerates quickly** when not using
✅ **Can't attack while blocking**
✅ **Movement slowed by 50%** while blocking
❌ **DON'T hold too long** - shield depletes!
❌ **Shield break = 2 sec stun** (DANGER!)

## ⚡ SMASH ATTACK SYSTEM

### What Are Smash Attacks?

- **Chargeable power attacks** that deal MORE damage
- Hold to charge up to **2.5x power**!
- Maximum charge at **2 seconds** (120 frames)
- Orange/Red charging visual with particles
- Longer cooldown than normal attacks

### Smash Attack Controls:

#### PS5 Controller:

- **HOLD R2 (Right Trigger)** = Charge smash
- **RELEASE R2** = Unleash smash attack!

#### Keyboard:

- **P1**: Hold **F** key, then release
- **P2**: Hold **.** (period) key, then release

### Charge Power Scaling:

```
No Charge (instant):  1.0x damage/knockback
Half Charge (1 sec):  1.75x damage/knockback
Full Charge (2 sec):  2.5x damage/knockback
```

### Damage Comparison:

| Attack Type              | Base Damage | Full Charge Damage |
| ------------------------ | ----------- | ------------------ |
| Normal Attack            | 12%         | N/A                |
| Smash Attack (no charge) | 12%         | 12%                |
| Smash Attack (half)      | 21%         | 21%                |
| Smash Attack (full)      | 30%         | 30%                |

### Smash Visual Indicators:

- **Orange glow** = Charging (0-1 sec)
- **Red glow** = Strong charge (1-2 sec)
- **Dark red glow** = MAX CHARGE (2 sec)
- **Orbiting particles** = Charging effect
- **Power multiplier** shown above head (1.5x, 2.0x, 2.5x)
- **Orange hitbox** when attack releases

### Smash Knockback:

At 100% damage:

- Normal Attack: `16.0` knockback
- Full Smash: `40.0` knockback (**2.5x stronger!**)

### Smash Strategy:

✅ **Charge when opponent is far away**
✅ **Full charge for KOs at high %**
✅ **Instant release for quick punish**
✅ **Can't move while charging** (vulnerable!)
❌ **Don't overcharge** - opponents can hit you!
❌ **Longer cooldown** (40 frames vs 30)

## 🎯 ADVANCED TACTICS

### Shield Techniques:

**1. Perfect Shield Timing**

- Block right before hit
- Saves shield health
- Opponent gets pushback

**2. Shield Poke**

- Attack opponent's shield repeatedly
- Force shield break
- Get free 2-second punish window!

**3. Shield Bait**

- Block to make opponent think you're scared
- Drop shield and counter-attack
- Mix up timing

### Smash Attack Techniques:

**1. Full Charge Punish**

- Opponent respawns with invincibility
- Start charging smash
- Release when invincibility ends = HUGE hit!

**2. Edge Guard Smash**

- Charge at edge
- Release when opponent recovers
- Instant KO!

**3. Mix-Up Release**

- Don't always full charge
- Half charge catches opponents off-guard
- Faster than they expect

**4. Bait & Punish**

- Start charging to bait approach
- Release early or cancel (jump away)
- Punish their whiffed attack

### Combo Examples:

**Basic Smash Combo:**

```
1. Hit with normal attack (launches)
2. They land on platform
3. Start charging smash
4. Release when they recover
5. MASSIVE knockback!
```

**Shield Break Combo:**

```
1. Hit opponent's shield 3-4 times
2. Shield breaks (they're stunned)
3. Start charging full smash
4. Release for maximum damage
5. Easy KO!
```

**Edge Guard Smash:**

```
1. Knock opponent off stage
2. Position at edge
3. Charge smash attack
4. Release when they reach ledge
5. Meteor spike or side kill!
```
