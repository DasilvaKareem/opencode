# 🚀 Aerial Attacks Update - NEW MOVES!

## ✨ What's New: Air-Only Special Moves

Your Smash Bros game now has **aerial attacks** that can only be performed while in the air!

### 🔺 Up Air (Up Aerial)

- **PS5 Controller**: Triangle button (△) **[AIR ONLY]**
- **Keyboard P1**: E key **[AIR ONLY]**
- **Keyboard P2**: Right Shift **[AIR ONLY]**
- **Effect**: Launches opponent straight UP vertically
- **Visual**: Yellow hitbox above your character
- **Best For**: Juggling opponents, KO off top of screen

### 🔽 Down Air (Down Aerial / Meteor Smash)

- **PS5 Controller**: Circle button (○) **[AIR ONLY]**
- **Keyboard P1**: Q key **[AIR ONLY]**
- **Keyboard P2**: Right Alt **[AIR ONLY]**
- **Effect**: SPIKES opponent downward (meteor smash!)
- **Visual**: Purple hitbox below your character
- **Best For**: Spiking opponents into the abyss, edge guarding

### ⚔️ Normal Attack (Still Available)

- **PS5 Controller**: Square (▢) / R1 / R2
- **Keyboard P1**: Space
- **Keyboard P2**: Right Ctrl
- **Effect**: Launches opponent diagonally
- **Visual**: Red hitbox in front of character
- **Works**: Both on ground AND in air


## 🎯 Attack Properties

### Normal Attack (Red)

- **Damage**: 12%
- **Knockback**: Diagonal launch
- **Direction**: Based on attacker position
- **Hitbox**: 60x50 in front of character

### Up Air (Yellow)

- **Damage**: 12%
- **Knockback**: Pure vertical launch
- **Direction**: Straight up (0°)
- **Hitbox**: 60x65 above character
- **Perfect For**: Building combos, upper KO

### Down Air (Purple)

- **Damage**: 12%
- **Knockback**: Downward spike (METEOR!)
- **Direction**: Straight down (180°)
- **Hitbox**: 60x65 below character
- **Perfect For**: Edge guarding, stylish KOs

## 🎓 Strategy Guide

### Aerial Combo Routes:

**Beginner Combo:**

1. Hit with normal attack (launch diagonally)
2. Jump after them
3. Up Air (△) to juggle upward
4. Repeat for multiple hits!

**Advanced Combo:**

1. Hit at low % multiple times
2. Launch them up
3. Jump and Up Air (△)
4. Fast-fall and Down Air (○) to spike!

**Edge Guard:**

1. Opponent recovering from below stage
2. Jump off stage
3. Down Air (○) for METEOR SPIKE!
4. Double jump back to safety

### When to Use Each Attack:

**Normal Attack (▢/Square):**

- Ground combat
- Safe spacing
- General purpose

**Up Air (△/Triangle):**

- Opponent above you
- Following up after launch
- Juggling combos
- Top screen KOs at high %

**Down Air (○/Circle):**

- Opponent below you
- Edge guarding recoveries
- Stylish meteor spikes
- Fast-fall pressure

## 🎨 Visual Indicators

When you attack, you'll see colored hitboxes:

- **RED** = Normal attack
- **YELLOW** = Up air (above character)
- **PURPLE** = Down air (below character)

This helps you see where your attack hitbox is!

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Air-Only Requirement:

- ❌ Triangle/Circle do **NOTHING** on ground
- ✅ Triangle/Circle **ONLY WORK** when airborne
- ✅ Must be in air (jumping, falling, off-platform)

### Attack Cooldown:

- All attacks share 30 frame cooldown (0.5 seconds)
- Can't spam attacks
- Strategic timing required

### Knockback Scaling:

- All attacks deal 12% damage
- Knockback scales with opponent's %
- Higher % = farther launch
- Down air can spike even at low %!

## 🏆 Advanced Techniques

### Aerial Combos:

```
Ground Attack → Jump → Up Air → Up Air → Up Air
(Low % combo string)

Ground Attack → Jump → Up Air → Down Air
(Spike finish)
```

### Edge Guarding:

```
1. Opponent launched off-stage
2. Run to edge
3. Jump off-stage
4. Down Air (○) to spike
5. Double jump back to stage
```

### Juggling:

```
1. Launch opponent upward
2. Follow with jump
3. Up Air (△) continuously
4. Keep them in the air
5. Finish with Down Air spike or let them fall
```
