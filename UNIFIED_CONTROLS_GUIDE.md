## 🕹️ DIRECTIONAL ATTACKS

### Just Press Square/Space:

- **Normal Attack** (red hitbox in front)
- Works on ground and in air
- Basic diagonal launch

### Hold UP + Press Square:

- **Up Air** (yellow hitbox above) **[AIR ONLY]**
- Launches opponent straight up
- Perfect for juggling

### Hold DOWN + Press Square:

- **Down Air** (purple hitbox below) **[AIR ONLY]**
- Meteor spike downward
- Devastating edge guard

### Hold Direction + Press Square:

- **SMASH ATTACK** (orange hitbox, starts charging!)
- Hold to charge up to 2.5x power
- Release Square to unleash
- Works with ANY direction (left, right, up, down)

## ⚡ HOW SMASH ATTACKS WORK

### To Perform Smash Attack:

1. **Hold a direction** (left stick or WASD/Arrows)
2. **Press AND HOLD Square/Space/RCtrl**
3. **Character starts glowing** (orange → red)
4. **Release button** to unleash the attack!

### Charging System:

- **Auto-charges** while you hold
- **0-1 sec**: Orange glow (1.0x - 1.75x)
- **1-2 sec**: Red glow (1.75x - 2.5x)
- **2 sec**: MAX CHARGE (2.5x power, auto-releases)
- **Power shown** above character (1.5x, 2.0x, 2.5x)

### Visual Feedback:

- Pulsing square outline around character
- Orbiting particles
- Color changes with charge level
- Power multiplier text

## 🎮 COMPLETE CONTROL SCHEME

### PS5 DualSense Controller:

```
MOVEMENT:
  Left Stick           = Move (all directions)
  D-Pad                = Alternative movement

JUMP:
  ✕ (X Button)         = Jump / Double Jump

ATTACK (All with Square ▢):
  Square               = Normal Attack
  Up + Square          = Up Air [AIR ONLY]
  Down + Square        = Down Air [AIR ONLY]
  Direction + Square   = SMASH ATTACK (hold to charge!)

DEFENSE:
  L1 or L2             = Shield Block

OTHER:
  START                = Restart

```

```
Down Air:   S + Space [AIR ONLY]
Smash:      Direction + HOLD Space, then Release

Block:      Left Shift
```

### Keyboard - Player 2:

```

Attack:     Right Ctrl
Up Air:     Up + Right Ctrl [AIR ONLY]
Down Air:   Down + Right Ctrl [AIR ONLY]
Smash:      Direction + HOLD Right Ctrl, then Release

Block:      Right Shift
```

## 💡 PRO TIPS

### 1. Tap vs Hold Square:

- **Tap Square** = Quick normal attack
- **Hold Square + Direction** = Charges smash attack!

### 2. Directional Control:

- **No direction** = Forward attack
- **Up direction** = Up air (in air) or up smash (ground)
- **Down direction** = Down air (in air) or down smash (ground)
- **Side direction** = Side smash

### 3. Charge Timing:

- **Instant release** = 1.0x damage (same as normal)
- **Half charge (1 sec)** = 1.75x damage
- **Full charge (2 sec)** = 2.5x damage + knockback!

### 4. When to Charge:

✅ Opponent recovering from off-stage
✅ After shield break (they're stunned)
✅ Opponent respawning (invincibility ending)
✅ When you have stage control

❌ Opponent close and mobile
❌ You're at high damage %
❌ In active neutral game
